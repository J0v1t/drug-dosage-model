# Educational pharmacokinetics

Language for the oral drug-concentration simulator and its research study.

## Language

**Hypothetical drug**:
An educational scenario with illustrative pharmacokinetic parameters, without claiming to represent a particular marketed drug.

**Real-drug example**:
An educational scenario tied to an identified oral drug and documented pharmacokinetic sources.

**Oral dosing**:
Administration through the gastrointestinal route, with absorption preceding entry into the modeled systemic compartment.

**One-compartment model**:
A simplified pharmacokinetic representation that treats systemic drug distribution as a single well-mixed compartment.

**First-order absorption**:
Transfer from the oral absorption depot at a rate proportional to the amount remaining there.

**First-order elimination**:
Removal from the systemic compartment at a rate proportional to the amount in that compartment.

**Published reference range**:
A source-defined concentration interval associated with a specified drug and study or labeling context; it does not establish universal safety.
_Avoid_: Safe zone

**Illustrative target band**:
A hypothetical concentration interval used to explore dosing behavior, without a real-drug efficacy or safety claim.

**Reference example**:
A real-drug teaching preset constructed from documented sources. Editing a parameter or dosing mode switches to a modified exploration.

**Sandbox exploration**:
An editable educational scenario, including a Theophylline-inspired exploration created automatically when a reference parameter or dosing mode is edited; changed pharmacokinetic parameters do not represent the sourced drug model.
